Collectively Made is a worker-owned cooperative of designers, engineers, and strategists. We build bespoke AI for organizations that can't find what they need off the shelf — quiet systems that close the gap without making your work feel like someone else's. The judgment comes from twelve years of shipping inside Fortune 500 engineering organizations; the nimbleness comes from not being one. We work like a lab: small, working things first, then we harden the ones that earn it.

What we build

Most AI gets bought and never shipped.

We build the part that has to work — and make it stable, observable, and yours. Same craft whether you're a startup, a non-profit, or a Fortune 500 team; the only thing that changes is the problem.

  • A tool your team actually opens — built around how you already work, not another tab to babysit.
  • The automation no vendor will build for just you: the copying-between-systems that quietly eats your week.
  • A narrow, trustworthy agent for one real task, with the judgment about what "good" means written in.

Selected clients

The honest answers.

Why is there no pricing on this page?

Because the right number depends on your problem, and we won't know it until we understand the work. The first conversation is where we scope that—not a sales call, a fit-read. Pricing for a specific build lives with that build.

Who actually does the work?

The practitioner who scoped it. We're worker-owned, so there's no account executive in the middle and no junior handoff. You work with the person building your system, start to finish.

What if AI isn't the right answer?

Then we'll say so. Sometimes the honest answer is that an off-the-shelf tool already covers it, or that a task shouldn't be automated yet. We'd rather lose the project than build you something that shouldn't exist.

How are you different from an AI agency?

Most AI shops are a product looking for a customer. We're a practice that happens to use these tools underneath, with judgment on top—what to build, what to refuse, what "good" means for you. Who owns what we build?

You do. The system, the data, the accounts it runs in—all yours. We build inside what you own, and we leave you able to maintain it.

What if we want to walk away?

You can. We work month-to-month once a build is live, with no long contract. You keep everything we made together. No prorated refund games.
